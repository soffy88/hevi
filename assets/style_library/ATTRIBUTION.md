# Attribution — handraw-style data

- Upstream: https://github.com/yang0/handraw-style
- Author: **yang0**
- License: **MIT License with Attribution Requirement**
- Used: 370 visual styles, 170 layout patterns, 36 theme colours (structured data only).
- Not used: the upstream `images/` sample gallery, QR codes, community links.
- Credit-removal policy: living or unconfirmed contemporary artist names are
  hidden; only style traits and non-personal labels remain. 184 style entries
  (178 unique reference names) are affected. The policy is stored as SHA-256
  hashes in `hevi/image/handraw_data/credit_policy.json`.
- Previews in this folder are drawn by HEVI (SVG), not copied from upstream.

Visible attribution appears in `NOTICE`, `/acknowledgements`, and the style
selector footer.
