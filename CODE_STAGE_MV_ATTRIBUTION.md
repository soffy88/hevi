# Code-stage MV graft: method attribution

## Source

Method only, reconstructed from an article *description* of a
three-part workflow (green-screen dance generation, code-built
stage with keying, and music mapped from dance events), as
summarised in `hevi-vs-code-stage-mv.md`. There is **no
repository, no workflow file and no reproducible engineering
artefact** behind that description, and no code, text or
configuration from any upstream project is copied in this
graft. The dance, stage and music ideas were re-implemented
from the description alone, in hevi's own gate / contract style.

## Numbers NOT verified, NOT used as thresholds

The article's self-reported numbers were not independently
verified and are not used anywhere in hevi as defaults or
acceptance thresholds: 38 dance action events, 20 music/action
sync nodes, 157 BPM, 5 final versions, 54 seconds / 15 shots,
about 55 seconds of storyboard. The grounding tolerance of
2 px in `qa.chroma_key_gate` is the tolerance stated in the
source report's keying table; every other threshold in the
three new/changed capabilities is a hevi-chosen default,
recorded in the gate report alongside the evidence, and must
be re-calibrated on real measurements before production use.

## Boundaries kept

- No account, key or call chain for any third-party video,
  music or chat service was created, used or assumed. The
  gates judge plans and measurements only.
- No third-party character image, dance video or finished
  music from the article was copied, imported or used as a
  demo. The 5-second chroma-key sample recorded in
  `/data/soffy/code-stage-sample/` (outside this repo) is
  synthetic material generated by code (a plain green
  background plus a simple solid-colour subject, composited
  onto a code-drawn stage background) and nothing else.
- The pure-code SVG skeleton dance route is not revived: the
  article describes it as a failed first attempt, and no
  skeleton-dance capability is added here.
- B2 (video action-event extraction), B3 (event-to-music /
  lighting wiring) and B4 (browser 3D stage renderer) were
  not built in this graft and remain residuals.

## What was built

- `qa.greenscreen_generation_gate` (A1): five greenscreen
  generation constraints on plan text, reusing the strict
  prompt-compiler anchor and provider-tier discipline.
- `pipeline.project_style_sheet` stage block (A2): lighting
  rig, camera path, particles and materials, under the
  existing version + stale discipline; incomplete stage plans
  stay `pending`.
- `qa.chroma_key_gate` (B1): six keying gates (edge, spill,
  grounding, shadow, proportion, lighting) over offline frame
  measurements, plus the FFmpeg chromakey contract (key
  parameters + command builder, no keying run inside the
  capability).

## B2 + B3 + B4 (2026-11-01 graft, same clean-room basis)

B2, B3 and B4 were built in a second graft on top of
`126b7fef`. The residual line above ("not built in this
graft") refers to the first graft only and is superseded by
this section.

- `qa.dance_action_events` (B2): footstep / turn / raise_arm
  / hold events from an offline keypoint contract only. No
  pose estimator is installed (no mediapipe dependency), no
  video is read for keypoints, and a bare video path fails.
  The only measured run uses code-generated synthetic video
  (a stick figure drawn from a fixed script, 320x240, 10fps,
  4.0s); its keypoints are the generator's own positions and
  the error table / F1 are scored against the script truth.
  Every report carries `human_measured: false` and the basis
  `synthetic_video`. Real-human measurement remains a
  residual: it needs a CPU pose estimator and a labelled
  human dance clip, neither of which exists here.
- `qa.event_trigger_wiring` (B3): versioned mapping table
  (footstep -> kick + ground light, turn -> transition +
  camera move, raise_arm -> accent + light pulse, hold ->
  pause + light hold), plan and offline artefacts only, no
  music generated. The tolerance is calibrated from measured
  dual-template runs (same events through two stage
  templates: identical trigger times, measured errors 0ms,
  calibrated tolerance 50ms, the hevi-chosen floor).
- `video.minimal_stage_render` (B4): minimal Three.js scene
  built from code with zero external assets, reusing the
  vendored Three.js build already in this repository (no
  download). Driven by `window.__hf` with settle
  confirmation and atomic capture under the determinism
  gates. Rendered twice per template with identical
  per-frame hashes (20 frames, 320x180, 10fps, 2.0s, two
  templates). Minimal version only: no Studio, no keyed
  video figure, no full stage system.
- Evidence for all three runs is recorded outside this repo
  in `/data/soffy/code-stage-sample-b2b3b4/` (synthetic
  video + error table, dual-template wiring evidence,
  double-render frame hashes), not in this repo.
- Boundaries kept in this graft too: no third-party video /
  music / chat account, key or call chain; no article
  material; no SVG skeleton dance; no self-reported article
  number used as a threshold or default.
